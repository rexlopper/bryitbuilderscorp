Upgrade-Led Capabilities

Upgrade Catalog

This catalog is organized around upgrade use cases: spaces that need better function, better presentation, better systems, or a deeper fit-out. These are representative capabilities and target scopes, not claimed completed projects.

Use it to understand where your project sits: quick refresh, business upgrade, occupied-site renovation, sustainable upgrade, or full design-build delivery.

Cafe & Restaurant Upgrades

Cafes & Restaurants

Interior upgrades, build-outs, and renovations for food and beverage spaces that need flow, brand presentation, kitchen coordination, and durable finishes.

Typical Budget: ₱500K - ₱3M

Typical Scope: dining area, counter, kitchen-facing coordination, lighting, finishes, signage support

Best For: new concepts, relaunches, and active businesses that need phased execution

Business Space Upgrades

Practical fit-outs and renovations for workplaces and customer-facing spaces where layout, presentation, and operations need to work together.

Typical Budget: ₱800K - ₱2.5M

Typical Scope: partitions, ceilings, lighting, front-of-house finishes, storage planning, MEP coordination
