The Firm

What We Bring

Family-Led Expertise
Built for Construction.

Bryit Builders Corporation is built around complementary strengths inside one family leadership group: engineering, data and analytics, team leadership, structural and civil execution, architecture and design direction, and occupational health discipline.

That mix matters in construction. It means design decisions, engineering judgment, operational discipline, and site responsibility are not treated as separate conversations, but as one coordinated way of building.

Engineering & Strategy

Rex

Rex brings technical depth and business strategy to Bryit Builders Corporation. He holds two master's degrees: a Master of Science in Electrical Engineering from UP Diliman and a Masters of Business in Innovation from the Asian Institute of Management. Combined with his background in data management and analytics, his profile strengthens the firm's ability to connect engineering rigor with modern decision-making.

Construction Delivery

Arcelo

Arcelo brings more than 30 years of construction experience shaped by structural and civil engineering expertise, field execution, and practical judgment. Health & Site Discipline

Brythel brings medical training from the University of Santo Tomas into a field where occupational health and site responsibility matter deeply. Her perspective reinforces worker welfare, health awareness, and a culture of discipline in construction environments.
