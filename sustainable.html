The Philippine Context

Sun, Heat, Plants,
and Rising Power Costs.

Sustainable construction in the Philippines is not only about certifications. It is about making spaces cooler, brighter, and more efficient in a hot, humid setting where electricity costs are high and air-conditioning loads can dominate the monthly bill.

Plant-forward interiors help soften hard commercial spaces, improve atmosphere, and support a more welcoming customer experience. Solar integration adds another layer of practicality by turning the country's strong sun exposure into long-term savings.

We treat sustainability as a design language and an engineering strategy at the same time, balancing visual warmth, tropical resilience, and measurable energy performance.

5.1 Peak Sun Hours / Day

A strong baseline for rooftop solar planning across much of the Philippine archipelago.

₱10+ Per kWh (MERALCO)

High utility rates make energy-efficient planning and solar payback more attractive.

RA 9513 - RE Act

Net metering support gives properly documented solar systems stronger long-term value.

20+ Typhoons Per Year

Tropical resilience matters, which is why passive cooling and durable detailing stay part of the plan.

Solar Installation

From Solar Planning
to Energy Savings.

In the Philippines, a useful solar installation starts with proper roof evaluation, energy usage review, and documentation. We make sure the system is matched to the property instead of oversold, then help clients move toward interconnection and long-term value.

01

Shadow & Load Analysis

Site visit, roof assessment, and energy consumption review to size your system correctly.

02

System Design & DOE Permit

Electrical design by a PRC-licensed electrical engineer, DOE permit filing, and utility coordination.

03

Installation & Commissioning

Panel mounting, inverter installation, wiring, and full system testing before grid connection.

04

Net Metering Application

We file your net metering application with MERALCO or your distribution utility under RA 9513 — so your excess generation earns, not wastes.

Biophilic Interior Design

Using Plants for

Biophilic design is about bringing nature into the way a space is planned, experienced, and remembered. For homes, offices, restaurants, and hospitality spaces, indoor planting can soften hard interiors and create a calmer, more thoughtful atmosphere.

In the Philippine setting, plant-led interiors also work well with tropical light, natural textures, and warm ventilation strategies. The result is a space that feels fresher, more grounded, and more aligned with the kind of lifestyle clients increasingly want.
