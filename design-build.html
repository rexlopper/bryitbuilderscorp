Beyond the Upgrade

When Upgrades
Become Builds.

Some upgrades need more than finishes. When the scope becomes a full fit-out, extension, or new build, Bryit carries the project from design coordination to construction and turnover.

What It Covers

The deeper path
for bigger upgrades.

Many Philippine upgrade projects start as a renovation, then grow into drawings, permits, MEP coordination, custom fit-out, or structural work. That is where design-build depth matters.

We run architectural planning, engineering development, permit support, procurement coordination, and site execution as one connected process. This gives upgrade-led projects a path to become full design-build work without losing accountability.

From site assessment and design studies to LGU permits, technical coordination, procurement, and final turnover, we keep the project moving with one point of responsibility.

How We Work

Our Six-Stage Delivery

01

Consultation & Site Assessment

We begin with a project brief, site review, target budget, and feasibility check so the architectural direction already reflects local realities before drawings begin.

02

Architectural Design

Our architects prepare schematic studies, floor plans, elevations, and 3D presentations tailored to tropical living, shading, ventilation, and the way Filipinos actually use space.

03

Structural & M/E/P Engineering

Structural, electrical, plumbing, and mechanical documents are coordinated early so the design is buildable, code-compliant, and ready for pricing and permit submission.

04

Permit Processing

We assist with the LGU and agency paperwork required for construction, helping clients move through barangay, fire, and occupancy-related steps with less friction.

05

Construction & Quality Control

The same team that planned the job stays involved during construction, which keeps materials, workmanship, and site decisions aligned with the approved design intent.

06

Turnover & Occupancy Permit

We close with punch listing, final checks, client walkthroughs, and turnover support so the completed building is ready for occupation and long-term use.
